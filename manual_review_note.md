# Manual review note

The paper reports a manual review of a random subsample of ten of the 150 main-sample conversations. This was an audit of the coding, not a second, independent human coding of those conversations.

The available methodological record does not provide a separate ten-case review log, an independent set of human turn-level labels, or paired human–LLM coding decisions. Accordingly, this release does not assert a detailed audit checklist, a selection seed for that subsample, a count of corrections, or a quantified agreement result. It also does not establish whether the ten randomly reviewed conversations overlapped with the three cases flagged by semantic validation.

The ten-case review must not be described as an inter-rater reliability study. A human–LLM inter-rater agreement coefficient cannot be reported from this audit. No kappa, alpha, percentage agreement, or other agreement metric is supplied or implied. Automated schema and semantic checks establish structural or internal consistency, not independent validation of the substantive interpretations.
